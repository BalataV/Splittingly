SUPPORT

Write to support@splittingly.com. Most answers arrive within a working day.

How do I join a group?

Ask a member to share the invite link, or type their six-character code under Join with a code. If you already have the app installed, the link opens straight into it.

Does Splittingly move money?

No. It records that a payment happened and updates the balance. The transfer itself happens between you and the other person, however you normally pay each other.

The app is in one language but my group counts in another currency

That is normal and intended. Language and currency are separate settings. The currency decides how amounts are formatted; the language decides everything else.

Why does a three-way split show different amounts?

Because the total does not always divide evenly. The remainder — one minor unit per person at most — goes to whoever paid, so the shares always add back up to the exact total.

How do I remove the ads?

One-time purchase under Profile → Remove the ads. No subscription. It applies to every group you are in, on every device signed into the same account.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Or use the web form. Expenses you created stay in their groups under "former member" so other people's balances stay correct.

Something is wrong with a balance

Open the expense and check its history — every edit records who changed what and when. If the numbers still look wrong, email us the group name and roughly when it happened.
