Split any way

Equally, by shares, or exact amounts. The remainder always goes to the payer, so nothing is lost.

Categories and stats

Where the money went, who spent most, and a playful year in review.

Push when it matters

New expense, settled debt, edited amount. Quiet hours included.

45 LANGUAGES.
YOUR CURRENCY.

Language and currency are separate settings. The app in Italian while a group counts in Thai baht is a normal state, not an edge case.

The currency decides the symbol side, the thousands separator, the decimal mark and whether there are decimals at all. Zero-decimal currencies — JPY, KRW, VND, ISK — drop the fraction everywhere, including the split maths.

Right-to-left languages mirror the whole interface. Amounts stay left-to-right inside the line, where they belong.

Forty-five languages are translated in full — every screen, every error, every line the two characters say. The picker lists fifty; the remaining five run in English until their translation lands.

HONEST ABOUT ADS

Splittingly is free and pays for itself with advertising. So we drew a hard line and put it in the design.

No ads where money is entered or confirmed. New expense, split editor, receipt capture, settle up, every success state and every sign-up screen stay commercially empty. That is not a setting; it is how the app is built.

Everywhere else, an ad is framed in a dashed grey box that appears nowhere else in the interface — so its shape alone reads as "not us".

One payment removes every ad. No subscription — Splittingly is used in bursts, and a monthly fee for that is a bad deal for you.

Pro also lifts the receipt-photo limit, unlocks the full stats history, a pie chart breakdown by category, every colour theme and CSV export. Everything that touches the group — unlimited groups, members, expenses, the settlement maths, invite links — is free for everyone, always. Nobody should get a worse app because somebody else did not pay.
